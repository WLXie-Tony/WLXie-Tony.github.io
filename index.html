01 / About

Curiosity, with a method.

I study the stories hidden in economic data — especially the words that move firms and markets.

The researcher

Wenlan (Tony) Xie

I am an Economics student at the University of Chicago. My work uses natural language processing and large language models to study information disclosure, corporate decisions, and investor attention.

Previously, I earned a Master of Commerce in Data Analytics from the University of Sydney with Distinction and a Bachelor's degree from the University of Canterbury.

Read what numbers miss.
